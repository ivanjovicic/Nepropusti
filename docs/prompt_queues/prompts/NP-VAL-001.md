# NP-VAL-001 — Serbian Open-Price Data Audit

## Goal
Determine whether Serbian open/official retailer price data can support trustworthy commerce Watch alerts.

## Scope
Audit at least 10–15 major retailer datasets and document license/reuse terms, format/update frequency, product identifiers, brand/title/unit/package, price fields, promotion dates, store granularity, stock if present, history availability, and stale/missing rates.

## Required output
- `data/source_audit.csv`;
- coverage/freshness summary;
- product-identity error examples;
- GO / LIMITED / NO-GO recommendation.

## Guardrail
Do not infer commercial-use rights from mere public visibility. Record exact license/source basis.
