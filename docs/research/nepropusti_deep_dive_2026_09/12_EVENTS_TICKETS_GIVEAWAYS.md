# Events, Tickets and Giveaways

## Events

Bandsintown and Songkick already solve:
- artist following;
- event discovery;
- concert reminders.

Generic event alerts are commodity.

## Ticket opportunity

Higher-value triggers:
- tickets on sale;
- new inventory;
- resale/returned allocation;
- reschedule/cancel;
- low inventory where partner exposes it.

The strongest version is partner-embedded, not another event directory.

## Ticket risk

Inventory state often belongs to a small number of powerful ticketing partners.
Without feed/webhook access, accuracy suffers.

## Giveaways

Useful for:
- SEO;
- acquisition;
- verified-trust content.

Weak as core because:
- low ARPA;
- variable legality/rules;
- prize campaigns expire;
- user quality can be incentive-driven.

Keep as later acquisition experiment only.
