# Market Size and Unit Economics

## Serbia ecommerce anchor

NBS reports for Q1 2026:
- 32.9M online purchases;
- 23.8M on domestic websites;
- 5,948 domestic online stores at end-March;
- domestic-store count +20.2% YoY.

NBS notes this excludes cash-on-delivery transactions.

This confirms a meaningful and growing ecommerce base.

## Platform direction

Third-party datasets consistently show WooCommerce as a major/dominant platform, but exact counts differ substantially due methodology.

Do not multiply third-party store counts directly into TAM.

## Bottom-up initial serviceable segment — scenario, not census

Start from NBS 5,948 stores.

Filter hypothetically:
- ecommerce categories where stock/variant Watch matters;
- enough traffic to create waitlist volume;
- lacks adequate internal solution;
- technically/politically able to integrate;
- willing to pay.

If only 2–5% qualify as strong near-term targets:
~120–300 potential Serbia partners.

This is a scenario, not measured SAM.
