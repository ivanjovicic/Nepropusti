# Partner ICP and Willingness to Pay

## First ICP

**Mid-market fashion/footwear merchant in Serbia.**

Characteristics:
- meaningful ecommerce traffic;
- stock/size churn;
- lost demand from unavailable variants;
- owner/ecommerce manager reachable;
- WooCommerce/custom stack;
- incomplete or underused item-level Watch automation;
- can expose one test product page quickly.

## Buyer

Likely:
- ecommerce manager;
- owner/founder for smaller merchant;
- digital/CRM lead for larger merchant.

## Why not biggest enterprises first

They are more likely to already have:
- CRM;
- app;
- loyalty;
- first-party data team;
- procurement/security overhead.

## Main objection

> “Why don't I install a €49–59 plugin or use my existing CRM?”

A good answer must be evidence-based:
- managed multi-trigger layer;
- less implementation/maintenance;
- better conversion attribution;
- waitlist/demand analytics;
- optional portable audience/channel;
- future cross-merchant consumer acquisition.

If the answer is only “nicer notification”, the business thesis fails.

## WTP evidence today

**None.**

Adjacent software proves merchants pay for ecommerce automation, but does not prove NePropusti WTP.

## Real WTP gate

Count only:
- paid pilot;
- signed commercial pilot;
- invoice/contract;
- clear budget-owner commitment tied to measured ROI.
