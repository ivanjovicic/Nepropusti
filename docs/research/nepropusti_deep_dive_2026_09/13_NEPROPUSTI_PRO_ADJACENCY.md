# NePropusti Pro Adjacency

## Hypothesis

Professional alerts for:
- grants;
- subsidies;
- public calls;
- tenders;
- scholarships;
- regulatory deadlines

may have materially higher willingness-to-pay than consumer price alerts.

The Ministry of Economy currently publishes numerous time-bounded public calls, including programs that close early when funds are exhausted.

## Why interesting

Missing one relevant call can have much higher economic cost than missing a product discount.

Analytical `DEADLINE_SOON` trigger score:
**7.77/10**

## Why not merge into consumer product now

Different:
- buyer;
- source set;
- terminology;
- trust requirement;
- onboarding;
- pricing.

## Recommendation

Run as a **separate adjacency memo/concierge test** only after the core merchant Watch validation begins.

Do not add grant/tender UI into the main NePropusti MVP.
