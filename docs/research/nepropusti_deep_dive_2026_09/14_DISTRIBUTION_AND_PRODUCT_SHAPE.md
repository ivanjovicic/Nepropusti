# Distribution and Product Shape

## Winning shape hypothesis

**Hybrid B2B2C**, but narrower than before:

`merchant product page -> hosted/embedded Watch -> user-requested service alert -> return to merchant`

Portable NePropusti identity is optional/later.

## Why merchant distribution still matters

Consumer price/discovery destinations already have strong incumbents.

The product should appear where intent already exists.

## WooCommerce opportunity

Third-party platform datasets disagree on exact counts/share, but all point to WooCommerce as a major/dominant Serbia ecommerce platform.

Examples:
- ShopRank June 2026: 62.7% of verified physical-delivery stores;
- StoreLeads Aug 2026: 10,155 live WooCommerce stores.

Methodologies differ. Do not treat either as official national market share.

## Plugin decision

Do not build a WooCommerce plugin first.

Sequence:
1. hosted link;
2. JS test surface;
3. 3 live merchant pilots;
4. only then plugin if integration repetition proves the need.

## Consumer destination

De-prioritize.

A destination app would fight:
- Tprice;
- Idealno;
- Cenoteka;
- ePonuda;
- Google;
- merchant apps.

## Native app

DROP from near-term scope.

## Browser extension

Later/fallback only.

## No-login watch

Recommended for pilot:
email -> verification -> active watch.

Account can be offered after value is delivered.
