# Price Truth Reassessment

## Verdict

**Price Truth should be demoted from product identity to supporting evidence/context.**

## Why

- Tprice already turns public price data into large-scale consumer comparison/history.
- Cenoteka already supports price/discount thresholds.
- Idealno/ePonuda already own comparison/discovery behavior.
- open-data access is non-proprietary.

## Still-useful price features

Inside a Watch:
- current price;
- threshold;
- observed history;
- 30-day context where legally/methodologically appropriate;
- unit price;
- source/freshness.

## Dangerous conflations

Do not treat as identical:
- advertised old price;
- legal previous-price concept;
- observed historical minimum;
- lowest market price;
- loyalty/member price;
- coupon-adjusted price;
- delivered total including shipping.

## Role in MVP

Price can be a second trigger once one merchant Watch surface works.

Do not build a national catalog/history product first.
