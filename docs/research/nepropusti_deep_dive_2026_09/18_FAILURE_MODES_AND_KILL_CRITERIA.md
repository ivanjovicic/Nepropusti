# Failure Modes and Kill Criteria

## 2029 pre-mortem

1. Tprice/Idealno/Cenoteka make consumer price Watch redundant.
2. Merchant installs a €49–59 plugin instead of NePropusti.
3. Merchants refuse third-party ownership of high-intent customer identity.
4. Hosted/embedded button adds conversion friction.
5. Users do not create watches even when offered at point of intent.
6. Restock alerts arrive after inventory is already gone.
7. Variant identity errors create wrong alerts.
8. Merchant stock webhooks are unreliable.
9. Public price data lacks stock/loyalty/shipping truth.
10. Cross-store entity matching creates support burden.
11. Partner sales cycle is too slow for €99–199 ARPA.
12. Merchant onboarding takes too many founder hours.
13. Cross-category portable identity does not improve retention.
14. Users consider one-off commerce watch complete and never return.
15. Sponsored messages corrupt trust in service alerts.
16. Direct-marketing/privacy handling becomes complex.
17. Sports broadcaster data reuse cannot be licensed cleanly.
18. Ticket platforms refuse inventory integrations.
19. Regional expansion requires rebuilding source operations per country.
20. Demand analytics is interesting but not budget-worthy.
21. Large merchants already have superior CRM/loyalty.
22. Small merchants have too little traffic to generate watch volume.
23. Generic browser monitoring creates false-state alerts.
24. Product expands into super-app before one paid wedge works.

## Structural risks

- trigger primitives are easy to copy/buy;
- merchant data ownership;
- country/source-specific operations;
- temporary commerce retention;
- incumbent comparison/CRM distribution.

## Avoidable risks

- building a super-app;
- owning customer identity too aggressively;
- crawling when merchant webhook is available;
- mixing service alerts with marketing;
- building native app early;
- confusing open data with moat.

## 30-day gate

Continue if:
- 20 strong-fit merchant conversations are underway/completed;
- ≥5 credible pilot commitments;
- first hosted/embedded test surfaces identified;
- at least 50 qualified user exposures planned or started.

Kill/narrow B2B2C if <2 real commitments after 20 strong-fit pitches.

## 60-day gate

Continue if:
- ≥3 live partner surfaces;
- one narrow trigger works reliably;
- materially wrong/stale alerts <5%;
- alert creates measurable return actions;
- onboarding/support burden is tolerable.

## 90-day gate

Promote investment only if:
- ≥3 paying partners or equivalent signed paid pilots;
- ≥30% qualified watch creation;
- ≥25% action rate on genuinely useful triggers is a strong candidate signal;
- partner can see attributable recovered value;
- at least two partners request continuation/expansion.

## Six-month gate

Must have:
- repeatable partner acquisition;
- recurring revenue;
- standardized integration path;
- clear gross-margin-positive support model.

If not, stop treating NePropusti as a scalable software company and either narrow to a service/tool or stop.
