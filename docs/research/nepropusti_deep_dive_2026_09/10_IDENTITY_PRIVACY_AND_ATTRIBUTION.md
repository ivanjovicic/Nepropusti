# Identity, Privacy and Attribution

## Recommended identity model

### Initial
**Merchant-owned relationship + NePropusti service processor.**

User can create a watch with:
- email;
- optionally Telegram/other delivery identity.

No mandatory NePropusti account.

### Later
Offer portable NePropusti identity if users demonstrate value from:
- multiple merchants;
- multiple active watches;
- second category.

## Why not force platform ownership

Partners may resist sending high-intent customer identities to a third-party consumer brand.

Users also do not need an account to understand:
`notify me when this happens`.

## Service alert vs marketing

Keep them separate.

### User-requested service alert
“Size 42 is available.”

### Marketing
“You may also like these shoes.”

Under Serbian rules, direct advertising via electronic messages requires prior consent. Do not smuggle sponsored marketing into a service alert.

## Interest graph

Watch history can reveal:
- brand preference;
- budget thresholds;
- sport/event interests;
- location/context.

Treat as personal/profiling data with minimization.

## Partner analytics

Prefer aggregate:
- number of active watchers;
- variant demand;
- threshold distribution where privacy-safe;
- conversion after trigger.

Never sell individual-level watch history as a default business model.

## Attribution

Use narrow first-party-style evidence:
watch -> alert -> return click -> optional merchant postback/order.

Avoid unnecessary third-party behavioral tracking.
