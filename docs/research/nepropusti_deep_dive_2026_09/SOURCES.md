## Evidence limitations
- Merchant audit is a 50-site public-source reconnaissance, not a logged-in/manual UX test of every merchant.
- `NOT_FOUND_PUBLIC` means the feature was not found in indexed/current public evidence; it does not prove the merchant lacks it.
- Platform-share sources disagree materially because their methodologies differ. Use them only directionally; NBS is the authoritative domestic-store/payment anchor.
- Raw retail CSV row sampling could not be reliably retrieved in the research runtime. Schema-level availability is verified; barcode completeness/duplicate rates remain an NP-VAL-001 empirical task.
- Revenue, ARPA, trigger and score values are analytical scenarios, not measured outcomes.
- Legal analysis is research, not legal advice.
