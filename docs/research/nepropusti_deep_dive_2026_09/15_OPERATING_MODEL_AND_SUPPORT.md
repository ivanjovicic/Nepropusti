# Operating Model and Support

## Pilot

Founder can run:
- partner interviews;
- hosted Watch;
- manual verification;
- email delivery;
- attribution sheet.

## 10 partners

Expected recurring work:
- onboarding;
- product/state integration;
- source troubleshooting;
- alert disputes;
- conversion reporting.

## 50 partners

A pure founder model becomes risky unless:
- integrations standardize;
- webhook/feed model dominates;
- partner self-service exists;
- alert-quality monitoring is automated.

## 100+ partners

Likely needs:
- partner success/support;
- integration/data operations;
- sales.

## Typical support tickets

- “alert never arrived”;
- “product was still unavailable”;
- “wrong size/variant”;
- “price is different at checkout”;
- “this was loyalty-only price”;
- “why did I get duplicate alert?”;
- unsubscribe/privacy;
- attribution dispute.

## Best operating-burden reducer

Use merchant-owned canonical IDs and webhooks for the first B2B wedge.

Do not solve universal cross-store identity before proving partner economics.
