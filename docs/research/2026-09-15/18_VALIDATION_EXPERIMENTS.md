# Validation Experiments

## E1 — Commerce watch

50–100 qualified users.

Each tracks max 3–5 concrete products.

Test:
- target price;
- restock;
- price-truth context.

Success hypotheses:
- >=30% of qualified visitors create a real watch;
- >=25% useful-alert action rate when a genuinely relevant trigger occurs;
- <5% false/stale materially wrong alerts.

Kill:
- <15% watch creation after clear proposition;
- users say current Idealno/Cenoteka flow is already sufficient.

## E2 — Sports where-to-watch

50–100 users, narrow entity set, four weeks.

Success:
- >=3 follows median among activated users;
- useful alert action on recurring fixtures;
- strong evidence that alert replaces search/checking.

Kill:
- broadcast mapping cannot be reliably sourced;
- users already rely on incumbent notifications and do not change behavior.

## E3 — Embedded partner widget

Pitch 20 highly relevant partners.

Success:
- >=5 real pilot commitments;
- >=3 actually publish hosted/embedded Watch surface.

Kill:
- <2 genuine commitments after 20 relevant pitches.

## E4 — Verified giveaway feed

Run verified-only Telegram/email/web feed.

Measure:
- acquisition;
- shares;
- return;
- cross-category migration.

Do not promote to core on pageviews alone.

## E5 — Event test

One segment only:
concert ticket alerts OR family weekend events.

## E6 — Cross-category test

Only after a wedge activates users.

Offer a second category.

Promote horizontal brand only if:
- meaningful cross-adoption;
- improved retention;
- no material increase in mute/unsubscribe.

## E7 — Paid partner test

After attributable actions exist, ask for actual payment.

No “would pay” surveys count as pass.
