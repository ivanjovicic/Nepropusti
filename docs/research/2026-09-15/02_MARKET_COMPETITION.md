# Market & Competitive Landscape

## Structural conclusion

NePropusti is entering an established behavior, not inventing one. Users already follow teams, artists, pages, prices, listings and flights. The opportunity is to unify **high-value state conditions** with better local data/trust and possibly partner distribution.

## Horizontal competitors

### Google

Google Alerts sends email when new web results match a topic. Google also stores followed interests and notifications, while product price tracking exists in selected countries and Google Flights tracks significant fare changes. This makes Google the most dangerous long-term platform competitor.

Serbia is not currently listed among Google's supported price-tracking countries, which creates a temporary local opportunity.

### Visualping / Distill

Both prove substantial demand for “watch this page”. Visualping now offers API, browser extension, Telegram/webhook/MCP integration. Distill reports 2M+ browser-extension downloads and 500k+ daily active users.

NePropusti cannot win by monitoring HTML more conveniently. It must understand structured entities and meaningful domain states.

## Commerce competitors

### Idealno

Reports ~1.45M products from 192 stores. Product pages show price movement and allow price-drop notification. This is a major incumbent.

### ePonuda

Reports 700+ merchant partners, 5M+ active offers, 55M+ annual visits across its network and CPC monetization. It is proof that merchants pay for high-intent comparison traffic.

### Cenoteka

Already allows users to define a desired price or discount percentage and receive a notification when a product meets that rule. This directly invalidates “target price” as sufficient differentiation.

## Sports competitors

SofaScore/Flashscore own deep match/live-score behavior. Flashscore now sends personalized news notifications around followed teams and players. NePropusti should not enter live-score depth.

## Event competitors

Bandsintown supports artist/venue/festival follows and has a mature five-stage lifecycle alert system for venues; its venue product cites an 80M+ fan network. Songkick performs artist concert tracking. Local eFinity/Tickets.rs own ticket transaction inventory.

## Giveaway competition

Local aggregators exist, but official regulator data creates a stronger trust anchor than generic reposting.

## Infrastructure, not product competition

OneSignal, FCM and Knock solve delivery/orchestration/preferences. They should be evaluated as build-vs-buy infrastructure, not imitated as the product moat.

## Competitive whitespace

The best remaining whitespace is not a blank feature cell. It is the combination:

**local trusted data + structured condition + cross-source entity state + embedded distribution + actionable alert + measurable partner outcome.**
