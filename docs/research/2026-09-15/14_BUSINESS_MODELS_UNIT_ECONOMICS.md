# Business Models & Unit Economics

## Most plausible ranking

1. B2B2C widget SaaS / usage / outcome fee
2. commerce CPC/CPA/affiliate where available
3. organizer/merchant promoted campaigns with strict separation from service alerts
4. B2B API / white label
5. consumer premium later

## Why partner-funded may win

Consumer willingness to pay for notification utility is unproven and likely low at Serbian price points. Partners can directly value return traffic/recovered conversion.

ePonuda is important evidence: it reports 700+ merchant partners and explicitly charges per click, demonstrating merchant willingness to pay for high-intent outbound traffic in this market.

## Illustrative partner economics

Not validated pricing:

- 50 partners × €49 = €2,450 MRR
- 200 × €59 = €11,800 MRR
- 500 × €79 = €39,500 MRR

This shows why €10k MRR is plausible only if B2B distribution becomes repeatable; it is not evidence that partners will accept these prices.

## Consumer premium

At €2.2/month:
- 10k MAU × 3% paid = €660 MRR
- 50k MAU × 5% paid = €5.5k MRR

Consumer premium alone likely needs large scale.

## Data-cost discipline

Key KPI:
`Data + processing cost / useful alert action`.

Sports licensed data and broadcast mapping can make a free consumer product structurally expensive. Open commerce data may produce better early unit economics.

## €10k MRR

Most credible route:
~150–250 paying B2B partners at tens of euros MRR plus qualified commerce/ticket traffic.

## €50k MRR

Serbia alone is much harder. It likely requires:
- hundreds of paying partners;
- substantial transaction/CPC volume;
- regional expansion;
- or strong white-label/API revenue.

Do not model €50k as a normal Serbia-only outcome.
