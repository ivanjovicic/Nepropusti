## Top five business-killing assumptions

1. Partners will actually embed and share outcome data.
2. The alert replaces meaningful checking behavior rather than adding another channel.
3. Source licensing/reuse permits reliable commercial monitoring at sustainable cost.
4. Alert accuracy/freshness can stay high enough that users trust the product.
5. Cross-category identity/watchlist creates incremental retention rather than confusion.

## Important counter-evidence

- Cenoteka already supports target price/discount criteria.
- Idealno already displays price history and price-drop alerts.
- Google already offers generic Alerts, interests, travel price tracking and price tracking in selected countries.
- Flashscore/Bandsintown already provide deep vertical notification systems.
- Halo Oglasi proves saved-search alerts are already native in listings.

Therefore “alerts” alone are not differentiation.
