# Risks, Kill Criteria & Pivots

## Pre-mortem — why NePropusti failed by 2029

1. Vertical apps were good enough.
2. Partner sites refused third-party watch identity.
3. Users created watches but rarely acted.
4. Alerts became noisy.
5. Price matching produced wrong products.
6. Open data covered the wrong retail segment.
7. Stock/coupon data remained unavailable.
8. Broadcaster data licensing was too expensive.
9. Sports rights changed too quickly.
10. Google launched better local tracking.
11. Cenoteka/Idealno copied trust/rule features.
12. Browser extension raised privacy concerns.
13. B2B sales cost exceeded MRR.
14. Consumer premium conversion was negligible.
15. Sponsored messages damaged trust.
16. Cross-category behavior was weak.
17. Generic brand hurt activation.
18. Regional data onboarding was country-by-country manual hell.
19. Giveaway traffic monetized poorly.
20. Event data required too much moderation.

## Whole-project kill criteria

Stop or pivot if after two focused wedges:
- neither creates repeated useful actions;
- no partner-financed path appears;
- data accuracy/freshness cannot meet a trustworthy standard;
- user research consistently says it adds another app rather than replacing checking.

## Pivot tree

Commerce works, partners do not:
-> B2C price-truth/affiliate tool.

Partners work, consumer brand weak:
-> white-label/API alert platform.

Sports works, commerce weak:
-> sports broadcast reminder vertical.

Giveaways acquire cheaply but do not retain:
-> use as SEO/acquisition only.

Professional deadlines show high willingness-to-pay:
-> separate NePropusti Pro.
