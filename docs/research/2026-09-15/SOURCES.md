## Limitations

- Vendor user counts, traffic, conversion/revenue claims and coverage figures are self-reported unless otherwise stated.
- Public price-data coverage is new and changing rapidly; dataset availability today does not prove long-term completeness.
- No broad Serbia-specific survey of alert fatigue, cross-category following or willingness-to-pay was found. These remain validation questions.
- Sports broadcaster data reuse rights remain a blocking unknown.
- Legal sections are research, not legal advice.
