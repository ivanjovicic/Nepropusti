# GTM & Partnerships

## Consumer GTM

Do not market “everything you can follow”.

Market one job:
- “Prava cena, bez stalnog proveravanja”
or
- “Kad igra i gde se gleda”.

Channels:
- SEO around high-intent trigger queries;
- niche communities;
- referral/share of a watch;
- partner-embedded acquisition.

## B2B2C GTM

First 20 partner prospects:
- mid-sized ecommerce shops lacking good price/restock alerts;
- ticket/event pages;
- niche sports publishers/clubs;
- brands running recurring campaigns.

Pitch:
> “We bring back users exactly when the condition they asked for becomes true, and we measure the return.”

Not:
> “We have a notification platform.”

## Partner pilot

No-code/hosted link first:
partner adds a simple `Watch` link or button.

Success:
- installs/publishes;
- real users create watches;
- partner receives attributed returns.

## Partnership before competition

For large aggregators (ePonuda/Idealno/Cenoteka), explicitly evaluate:
- deeplink/affiliate;
- data partnership;
- embedded integration;
- ignore;
before deciding to recreate catalog/data.

## Regional

Architecture can be regional; GTM/data cannot. Each country needs local sources/partners/rights.
