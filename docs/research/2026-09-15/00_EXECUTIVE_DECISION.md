# NePropusti — Executive Decision
Research cut-off: 2026-09-15

## Verdict

**VALIDATE BEFORE BUILDING**

The broad thesis survives desk research, but the strongest version is not “one app with sport + deals + giveaways + events”.

The most promising architecture/business thesis is:

> **NePropusti is a structured Watch platform: a user or partner declares what state change matters, NePropusti observes trusted sources, detects the transition, verifies it, and sends one actionable alert.**

## Best first wedge

**Price Truth / verified commerce alerts** currently ranks highest because Serbia has a new structural advantage: large retailers are now required to publish machine-readable, frequently updated price lists, and the open-data portal exposes datasets with identifiers such as brand/barcode/unit and price information.

This is not enough on its own: Idealno, ePonuda and Cenoteka are already strong. The wedge is therefore **not price comparison**. It is:

- target condition;
- trusted historical context;
- real-price interpretation;
- user-controlled watch;
- action notification.

## Best product shape

**Embedded Watch Button / Hybrid B2C+B2B2C**.

Example:

`[🔔 Obavesti me kada cena padne / kada se vrati na stanje / kada karte krenu]`

The partner keeps the transaction; NePropusti owns the watch/rule/alert relationship and proves attributable return traffic or recovered conversion.

This product shape scored above a standalone B2C destination because it can solve acquisition through partner distribution.

## Strong supporting wedge

**Sport + where-to-watch** remains a strong retention candidate.

Do not compete with SofaScore/Flashscore on live scores. Test:

`team/player -> fixture/time change -> Serbian broadcaster/stream confirmed -> reminder -> watch action`

The blocking unknown is broadcaster-rights/schedule data licensing and freshness.

## Giveaways

Useful as an acquisition/trust vertical, not proven as the core business. Serbia's regulator publishes organizers and campaign periods for regulated promotional prize games, creating an unusually good verification anchor.

## Events

Useful, especially ticket lifecycle and selected family/local use cases, but local ticketing platforms and global artist-follow products already solve much of the category. Start narrow or partner.

## What not to build now

- super-app;
- native mobile app;
- live sports scores;
- ticket marketplace;
- million-product catalog clone;
- generic AI chatbot;
- arbitrary web scraper network;
- all-country regional launch;
- public social feed.

## North Star candidate

**Useful Alert Actions per Weekly Active Follower**

A useful action is vertical-specific: merchant click, stream/broadcast action, ticket action, calendar add, verified giveaway rules/entry action, etc.

## Investment view

I would fund **8–12 weeks of structured validation**, not 12 months of product development.

Continue if:
- users create watches voluntarily;
- alerts generate meaningful action;
- at least 5 partner sites are willing to run an embedded pilot;
- partner ROI can be measured;
- data correctness is operationally sustainable.

Stop/pivot if:
- NePropusti is merely “one more app” and does not replace checking behavior;
- partner sites will not integrate;
- structured source rights cannot support reliable alerts;
- false/stale alerts are frequent enough to damage trust.

# IF THIS WERE MY MONEY

**Would I invest the next 12 months?** ONLY AFTER SPECIFIC VALIDATION.

**One problem:** users repeatedly check sites because they care about a future state change, not the current page.

**One first user:** a Serbian consumer waiting for a concrete commerce condition (price/restock) or recurring sports broadcast event.

**One trigger:** a high-confidence state transition crossing a user-created condition.

**One product shape:** embedded Watch Button feeding a cross-site NePropusti watchlist.

**One distribution channel:** partner embedding.

**One monetization hypothesis:** partner-funded SaaS/usage/qualified-action model; keep consumer core free during validation.

**Strongest moat candidate:** partner integrations + normalized entity/state history + trusted local source graph + accumulated watch/action data.

**Largest risk:** vertical incumbents remain good enough and partners see no reason to outsource the watch relationship.

**Experiment next week:** recruit 5 commerce/event partners for a no-code/manual Watch Button pilot and separately recruit 50 commerce-watch users.

**Continue metric:** >=5 real partner pilot commitments and >=30% of qualified users who understand the proposition create at least one real watch.

**Stop metric:** <2 genuine partner commitments after 20 highly relevant pitches, or <15% watch creation among qualified users after proposition/UX is clear.

**VALIDATE BEFORE BUILDING**
