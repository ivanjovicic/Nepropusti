# Privacy, Legal & Security

## Privacy

Serbian data-protection law explicitly includes interests, preferences, behavior and location within the concept of profiling.

The NePropusti interest graph is therefore personal data when linked to an identifiable user.

Requirements:
- data minimization;
- explicit purpose;
- transparency;
- deletion/export;
- secure access;
- objection/consent handling;
- separate treatment for direct marketing.

## Service vs commercial alerts

A user-requested alert (“tell me when my price target is reached”) should be clearly distinguished from sponsored promotion.

Serbian Consumer Protection law prohibits direct advertising by electronic communication without prior consent. Advertising law and e-commerce rules also require consent for direct commercial messages.

Do not sneak sponsored messages into transactional/service alert channels.

## Location

Prefer user-selected city/radius over background GPS.

## Children

Do not create child profiles in MVP. Family-event preferences should belong to adult accounts.

## Browser extension

Minimize host permissions. Explain exactly what pages/data are read. Avoid broad browsing-history collection.

## Security

Threats:
- malicious partner webhook;
- fake merchant/organizer;
- source poisoning;
- phishing links;
- account takeover;
- notification spam;
- browser-extension abuse;
- admin misuse.

Use signed partner webhooks, domain verification, audit history and external-link safety checks.
