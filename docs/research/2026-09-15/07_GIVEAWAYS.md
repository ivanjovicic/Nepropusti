# Verified Giveaways

## Opportunity

The giveaway wedge is stronger as a **trust/acquisition** product than as the initial monetization core.

The Serbian Games of Chance Administration publishes a 2026 list of organizers of promotional prize games in goods/services and their campaign periods. This gives NePropusti an official verification anchor that generic social repost pages do not have.

## Taxonomy

Do not mix:
- regulated promotional prize games;
- skill competitions;
- social-media giveaways;
- guaranteed gifts/promotions;
- gambling/betting.

## Product model

Fields:
Organizer, Prize, ValueEstimate, Eligibility, PurchaseRequired, EntryMethod, Deadline, DrawDate, RulesUrl, VerificationStatus, LastVerifiedAt.

## Alerts

- new verified match;
- deadline soon;
- material rules change;
- winner publication if useful.

## Trust

Never show invented win probability.

Potential `Opportunity Score` may combine:
- value;
- effort;
- purchase requirement;
- remaining time;
- verification.

It must not imply probability.

## Strategic role

Test as:
- SEO/share acquisition;
- cross-category activation;
- brand/organizer B2B campaign distribution.

Kill as a core vertical if repeat use and commercial value are low.
